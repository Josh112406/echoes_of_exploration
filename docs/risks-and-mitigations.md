# Risks and Mitigations

## Risk scale

- Probability: Low / Medium / High.
- Impact: Moderate / Major / Critical.
- Owner is responsible for early warning and mitigation, not solely for fixing it.

## Prioritized register

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Owner | Prevention | Trigger and response |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nine encounters exceed 12 hours | High | Critical | M1/M3 | Three reusable families; 2–3 minute scope; Hour 4 freeze | Hour 4 route missing → reuse accessible variant and cut decorative complexity |
| Late integration conflicts | High | Major | M2 | File ownership, short branches, hourly merges | Shared-file overlap → stop edits and use conflict protocol |
| Unsourced or misleading science | Medium | Critical | M4 | Claim ledger, primary sources, second reviewer | Claim lacks authority → remove or qualify before merge |
| Active mission status becomes stale | Medium | Major | M4 | Internal review date and release recheck | Source changes → update record and encounter copy |
| Unlicensed/third-party media | Medium | Critical | M5/M6 | Asset ledger, item-level rights check | Rights unclear → replace with team art or cleared NASA asset |
| Generated art mistaken for evidence | Medium | Major | M5 | Visible illustration labels and authentic archive media | Ambiguity in review → relabel/redesign/remove |
| Canvas blocks accessibility | High | Critical | M2/M6 | DOM alternative designed with each mechanic | Alternative missing at Hour 4 → block encounter merge |
| Old laptop performance fails | Medium | Major | M2/M5 | 2D assets, lazy chapters, budgets, reduced effects | Below 30 FPS → reduce effects/atlas size and use discrete variant |
| Mobile layout/input breaks | Medium | Major | M5/M6 | 360 px design and touch from vertical slice | Essential control clipped → freeze polish and repair layout |
| Local save corruption/data loss | Low | Major | M2/M6 | Versioned schema, validation, recovery, export | Invalid save → preserve original, offer recovery/reset |
| Runtime API or host outage | Low | Major | M2 | Bundled content and two compatible hosts | Primary host fails → deploy same static artifact to alternate |
| Assistant output introduces error/IP issue | Medium | Critical | Task owner | Availability check, bounded prompt, provenance, human review | Evidence/rights missing → reject contribution |
| Team skills do not match roles | Medium | Major | M1 | Hour 0 skill check and controlled swaps | Owner blocked after 30 min → reassign while preserving ownership log |
| Policies and submission left to end | High | Major | M1/M6 | Skeletons Hour 0; gates Hour 7/9 | Incomplete Hour 7 → stop nonessential art/content |
| No learner evaluation | Certain | Moderate | M1 | Make no effectiveness claim | Pitch implies measured impact → revise to design intent |
| Challenge resource links arrive late | Medium | Major | M4 | Ingest Resources tab at Hour 0 | Missing required dataset → prioritize challenge-provided resource integration |

## Fallback order

When time or performance fails, cut:

1. Decorative particles, parallax layers, camera effects, and unique transitions.
2. Audio variety and nonessential ambient layers.
3. Deep Dive length while retaining core evidence and source.
4. Broader catalog from 30 toward 25 groups.
5. Visual complexity inside an encounter; use the accessible discrete-choice version.

Preserve:

- Nine encounter starts, meaningful actions, results, and sources.
- Complete campaign opening and ending.
- Authentic evidence media and provenance.
- Keyboard/touch and non-canvas access.
- Local save/reset and policies.
- Privacy, security, licensing, and source integrity.

## Stop-ship conditions

- Any central fact cannot be sourced or is materially disputed without qualification.
- Any asset lacks redistribution rights or required credit.
- Any encounter has no keyboard/touch or accessible alternative.
- Progress can be uploaded or tracked contrary to the privacy claim.
- Campaign cannot finish from a clean session.
- Source, privacy, copyright, accessibility, or credits page is missing.
- Production deployment differs materially from the tested release candidate.
