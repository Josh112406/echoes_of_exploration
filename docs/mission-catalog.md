# Mission Catalog Plan

## Inclusion rule

The catalog covers major NASA mission groups whose hardware remains on a surface, impacted a body, remains in orbit, or continues/traveled into deep space. Selection balances:

1. Science enabled by the hardware.
2. Historical or engineering significance.
3. Destination, decade, outcome, and hardware diversity.
4. A clear source trail and explainable present status.
5. Relevance to the supplied challenge statement.

It is not a bolt-by-bolt inventory and does not claim literal completeness. Associated stages, instruments, landers, and rovers are grouped under a mission unless one receives a featured encounter.

## Status vocabulary

Use only controlled labels:

- `active-primary`
- `active-extended`
- `passive-science`
- `completed-surface`
- `completed-orbit`
- `silent-location-known`
- `silent-location-approximate`
- `intentionally-impacted`
- `unintentionally-impacted`
- `lost-or-unknown`

Display friendlier text, but retain the controlled value in content records. Store an internal review date even though the interface shows sources rather than “verified as of.”

## Publishing rule

An entry cannot ship until it has: a primary source, concise purpose, hardware list, destination/location description, status, science result, at least one connection, authentic-media provenance, internal review date, and named human reviewer.
