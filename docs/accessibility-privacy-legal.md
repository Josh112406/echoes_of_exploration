# Accessibility, Privacy, and Legal Plan

## Accessibility target

Target WCAG 2.2 Level AA for the surrounding interface and provide equivalent access to essential canvas content. A canvas-only game cannot meet the product requirement.

## Required accessibility behaviors

### Perceivable

- Text alternatives for authentic images, illustrations, maps, and meaningful icons.
- Captions/transcripts for speech or mission audio; no required information in sound alone.
- Contrast-compliant text and controls; color is never the sole state cue.
- Text-size controls and layouts that tolerate browser zoom.
- Reduced-motion mode and no rapid flashes.
- Authentic image versus illustration labels remain visible.

### Operable

- Complete keyboard operation with visible focus.
- Touch targets sized for mobile use.
- No essential hover, precision drag, multi-touch, or reaction-time barrier.
- Pausable timers; untimed/assisted alternative.
- Skip repeated navigation and return-to-map controls.
- Focus trapping/restoration for dialogs and overlays.

### Understandable

- Consistent control placement and plain-language instructions.
- Consequences explained after soft failure.
- Technical vocabulary defined at first use.
- Error messages say what happened and how to recover.

### Robust

- Semantic headings, landmarks, buttons, forms, and status announcements.
- DOM-based catalog and encounter alternative.
- Test with keyboard and at least one screen reader path.

## Privacy policy content

The policy should state plainly:

- No account, backend, telemetry, advertising, tracking pixel, or analytics cookie.
- Optional call sign, progress, achievements, bookmarks, and settings stay in browser storage.
- Nothing is transmitted by the game.
- Save export occurs only when the player chooses to download it.
- Import reads only the chosen file locally and validates it.
- View/reset/delete controls are available from settings.
- Clearing browser data or using private mode can erase progress.
- External source links leave the site and follow the destination’s policy.
- The hosting provider may keep operational request logs outside the game team’s control; link to the selected host policy in the deployed notice.

Do not collect a child’s age, school, email, location, free-form notes, or identifiable profile information.

## Copyright and licensing

### Team-created work

- Application code: Apache License 2.0.
- Original prose, normalized factual data, and team-created visual/audio assets: CC0 1.0.
- Record contributors and original-source boundaries.

### NASA media

NASA content is generally available for educational/informational reuse, but:

- Credit NASA and the specific center/mission/photographer where supplied.
- Check every item for third-party copyright.
- Do not imply NASA endorsement.
- Treat insignia, logotype, identifiers, and branding separately from public-domain-like media.
- Avoid promotional use of identifiable people without rights review.
- Preserve original captions when scientific interpretation depends on them.

Reference: [NASA Images and Media Usage Guidelines](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/).

### Space Apps terms

The team must hold rights to the submitted solution. Original work must be freely available or open source under the event terms. Do not submit proprietary, sensitive, or personally identifiable information. Reference: [2026 Participant Terms](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/legal/).

### Third-party and partner media

- Partner-agency material retains partner terms.
- Stock, font, icon, music, and sound-effect licenses must permit redistribution in an open submission.
- No “free download” asset without a recorded license.
- Huygens media and claims require ESA/partner credit where applicable.

## AI-assisted content

- Record tool, exact model, prompt purpose, output, reviewer, rights status, and disclosure.
- Authentic artifact evidence must not be replaced by generated imagery.
- Generated support art is labeled “illustration” and reviewed for misleading details.
- Never clone a real person’s voice or fabricate historical mission audio.
- Human review is mandatory for facts, educational explanations, legal text, and final assets.

## Required public pages

1. Privacy.
2. Copyright, license, and attribution.
3. Sources and methodology.
4. Accessibility statement and known limitations.
5. Credits, including assistant-disclosure summary.

## Legal acceptance gate

Every shipped asset appears in the asset ledger. Every required credit appears in-product. The repository includes Apache-2.0 and CC0 notices. The interface avoids implying NASA sponsorship or endorsement beyond participation in the challenge.
